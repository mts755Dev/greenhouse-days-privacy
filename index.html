Privacy Policy

Greenhouse Days
Last updated: November 20, 2026
Developer: mts755Dev

This Privacy Policy explains how Greenhouse Days (“the App”, “the Game”) handles information when you play on Google Play, the web, or other platforms where we publish it. We aim to keep the game simple and respectful of your privacy.

1. Who we are

Greenhouse Days is developed and published by mts755Dev. For privacy questions, open an issue on this repository or contact us through the store listing support channel.

2. Information we collect

The game itself is designed to collect as little as possible.

  • Game progress (on your device): save data such as day progress, coins, unlocked seeds, garden layout, and settings may be stored locally on your device (for example using browser or app local storage). This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or storage.
  • Settings: preferences such as graphics quality or mute state may be stored locally.
  • No account required: the game does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details inside the game.

3. Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect:

  • precise location
  • contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access for gameplay
  • government ID or sensitive personal information
  • children’s personal information for profiles or marketing lists

4. Advertising and platform services

Depending on where you play, advertising or platform SDKs may be present (for example ad networks used by HTML5 portals such as Y8, or advertising frameworks used on Google Play distributions).

Those third parties may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, interaction data, and diagnostic information to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and comply with law. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

If ads are shown in your version of the game, you may see privacy / consent options required by your region (for example GDPR or Google Play advertising disclosures).

5. How we use information

Local game data is used only to:

  • save and restore your garden progress
  • remember settings
  • operate core gameplay features

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use on-device save data for interest-based advertising beyond what third-party ad SDKs may do under their own policies when ads are enabled.

6. Sharing of information

We may share information only in these limited cases:

  • with platform providers (Google Play, hosting/CDN, ad partners) as needed to distribute the game
  • if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • to protect the security and integrity of the game or users

7. Data retention

Local save data remains on your device until you delete the app, clear storage, or reset progress in-game where available. We do not operate a central player profile database for Greenhouse Days.

8. Children’s privacy

Greenhouse Days is a general-audience casual game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a platform feature connected to the game, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address it.

If a specific store listing is rated for a younger audience, we will configure ads and data practices to match that store’s family / children policies.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate for a lightweight game. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Please keep your device updated.

10. Your choices

  • Clear local game data from your device/app settings to remove on-device saves.
  • Use platform privacy controls (Google Play / device settings) to limit ads personalization where available.
  • Uninstall the app at any time.

11. International users

The game may be offered worldwide through app stores and web portals. If you play from outside the country where the developer is located, your information may be processed in other countries by platform and advertising partners.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
